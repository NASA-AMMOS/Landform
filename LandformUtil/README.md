This subproject compiles to LandformUtil.exe and contains the subcommand drivers comprising the [Landform utilities](https://github.jpl.nasa.gov/OnSight/Landform/wiki/Command-Workflow#landformutilexe).

Subcommands here may or may not use either a local or cloud PipelineCore instance to provide database, storage, logging, and messaging.

Also see ../Landform and ../TilingServer.
